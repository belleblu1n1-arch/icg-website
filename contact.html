Frequently asked questions

What’s the easiest way to reach us?

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram — or use the form above. We respond within two business days.

What does the first conversation look like?

A quick exchange to understand your situation and see if there’s a fit. No preparation needed, no sales pitch. If a call makes sense, we’ll arrange one from there.

How are engagements structured?

We tailor scope to your business context — either as an ongoing retained model or a focused engagement around a specific need, typically 4–12 weeks. No long-term lock-in; you can scale support up or down as needs change.

How quickly can an engagement begin?

Most engagements start within two weeks of agreement. Focused engagements can begin sooner depending on scope.
