## Context

Island Capital Group (ICG) is building a premium static HTML/CSS/JS website for a senior advisory firm targeting CEO/leadership teams across Asia-Pacific. The site had evolved through multiple sessions with accumulated inconsistencies: old service taxonomy (ESG Compliance, Corporate Structure, Business Consulting), mixed navigation systems, duplicate A/B version sections, and misaligned vocabulary across pages.

This session focused on:
1. Finalising the homepage section architecture
2. Completely rebuilding the Services page
3. Running a sitewide consistency and cleanup pass
4. Preparing the site for publish

## Service Architecture — Final Decision

### The Problem
The old site used a flat 4-service model (Fractional CFO, ESG Compliance, Corporate Structure, Business Consulting). The new positioning needed a clear hierarchy with one primary engagement model and four focused service areas.

### Final Approved Structure
1. **Fractional CFO & Performance Leadership** — Primary engagement model (visually dominant)
2. **Lean & Stakeholder-Ready Reporting** — Focused service area
3. **Performance Management & Decision Support** — Focused service area
4. **Structuring, Governance & Cross-Border Readiness** — Focused service area
5. **Strategic Execution & Regional Expansion** — Focused service area (project-based)

### Key Decision: "How we help" vs Service Lines
There was significant discussion about whether the homepage "How we help" section should describe an abstract approach (insight ? decisions ? alignment ? execution) or directly reflect the service lines.

**Initial approach**: 4 abstract approach blocks mapping loosely to services.
**Problem identified by user**: Blending the overarching Fractional CFO description (which is the primary service) with secondary service language created confusion. The old version's CFO description was about the primary overarching offer, not a peer service.
**Final decision**: The "How we help" section was restructured as a value/impact table showing 5 rows — each mapping an area of impact to a delivery method. This directly reflects the 5 service lines without the abstract approach layer.

## Services Page — Complete Rebuild

### Design Direction
The page was rebuilt from scratch following a detailed editorial design spec. Key principles:
- Light, elegant, spacious, premium, editorial
- One clearly featured primary service, four elegant secondary cards
- Pill/chip tags for scope examples (not bullet lists)
- Generous whitespace, refined serif headings, soft sans-serif body
- No five-equal-service-essays treatment

### Section Structure
1. **Hero** — Eyebrow, serif headline ("Retained CFO leadership or focused support where it matters most"), subheadline, supporting line, CTA
2. **How clients engage us** — Two-column: left text + right panels (primary model panel + focused areas list)
3. **Featured primary service** — Full-width warm panel with label, title, fit line, body, "Best fit when" bullets, scope chips, outcome line, CTA
4. **Focused service cards** — 2x2 grid, each with title, fit line, body, best-fit-when, scope chips, outcome
5. **Why clients choose this model** — 2-column value points
6. **Closing CTA** — Centered narrow block

### CSS Architecture
All new Services page styles use the `sp-` prefix to avoid conflicts with existing homepage styles. Typography follows the spec: Playfair Display for headings, Source Sans 3 for body, with specific size/weight/colour hierarchies.

### Anchor IDs (for cross-page linking)
- `#fractional-cfo-performance-leadership`
- `#lean-stakeholder-ready-reporting`
- `#performance-management-decision-support`
- `#structuring-governance-cross-border-readiness`
- `#strategic-execution-regional-expansion`

## User Preferences Established

### Content Changes
- **Always ask before changing content** — user was upset when logos were reduced from 11 to 6 without asking
- **Never rename the misspelled filename** "consolidatied" — it's intentional (original filename)

### Vocabulary Rules
**Prefer**: senior, premium, practical, cross-border, outcome-oriented, decision support, performance management, prioritisation, execution, credibility, confidence, growth, complexity, change

**Avoid**: visibility, discipline, generic phrases like "innovative solutions", audit-heavy framing, overly technical language, CFO outsourcing language

### Design Rules
- Do not treat all 5 services as equal — maintain primary/secondary hierarchy
- Do not use accounting-firm menu style or admin/compliance-first grouping
- Aim for calm, premium, senior-advisory tone
- Use credibility strips to reinforce trust but keep visually integrated
