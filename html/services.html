Focused service areas

Focused support for businesses that need depth in a specific area.

Lean & Stakeholder‑Ready Reporting

For businesses that need reporting that is easier to produce, easier to use, and more credible with boards and external stakeholders.

Simplifying reporting and producing clear, relevant financial information for boards and external stakeholders — strengthening communication, credibility, and confidence.

Best fit when
  • Reporting feels heavy, fragmented, or too manual
  • Board and external stakeholder communication needs to improve
  • Financial information is not clear enough for the audience and purpose
  • The business needs stronger reporting quality without adding unnecessary process
Typical scopes include
  • Board pack redesign
  • Monthly reporting simplification
  • Reporting calendar reset
  • Management reporting templates
  • Stakeholder‑ready reporting improvements

Simplified reporting. Clear, relevant financial information. Stronger credibility and confidence.

Performance Management & Decision Support

For leadership teams that need finance to do more than report history.

Integrated and proactive performance management across target setting, forecasting, scenario planning, dashboards, risks and opportunities, accountability, and reward mechanisms — linking business drivers to financial impact and measurable delivery.

Enabling proactive course correction, aligned focus, and stronger team collaboration.

Best fit when
  • Priorities are set, but delivery is uneven
  • Planning and forecasting are too static or disconnected from operations
  • Teams need clearer accountability and follow‑through
  • Leadership needs stronger management cadence and earlier course correction
Typical scopes include
  • Rolling forecast design
  • Scenario planning
  • KPI framework design
  • Dashboard implementation support
  • Risk and opportunity tracking

Stronger performance delivery aligned to priorities.

Structuring, Governance & Cross‑Border Readiness

For businesses expanding, reorganising, or managing increasing cross‑border complexity.

Putting fit‑for‑purpose foundations in place to support growth and help manage increasing complexity and risk.

This includes structures, governance, and cross‑border readiness that support growth, change, and multi‑market operations without creating unnecessary drag.

Best fit when
  • The business is entering new markets or adding new entities
  • Governance and structures have not kept pace with growth
  • Cross‑border operations are becoming more complex
  • Leadership needs clearer foundations around expansion, risk, and coordination
Typical scopes include
  • Entity structure review
  • Governance and decision‑rights design
  • Cross‑border operating alignment
  • Expansion readiness support
  • Restructuring coordination

Stronger foundations for growth and cross‑border operations.

Strategic Execution & Regional Expansion

For leadership teams navigating major initiatives, transition, execution gaps, or regional change.

Providing practical support for restructuring, integration, market entry, productivity programs, regional expansion, and other major initiatives.

This is typically project‑based support for periods where priorities are clear, but delivery needs stronger structure, coordination, and follow‑through.

Best fit when
  • Execution is lagging behind strategy
  • The business is moving through integration, restructuring, or transition
  • Regional or cross‑functional initiatives need stronger coordination
  • Major change programs need experienced support to maintain momentum
Typical scopes include
  • Market entry support
  • Integration support
  • Restructuring support
  • Initiative tracking cadence
  • Regional coordination support

Support for strategic initiatives and complex change.
