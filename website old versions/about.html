Our Story

Fortune 500 Rigour, Mid-Market Agility

Island Capital Group was founded to bridge a gap in the market: mid-market companies expanding into Asia-Pacific need the same calibre of financial leadership that Fortune 500 corporations take for granted — at a scale and cost that makes sense for growing businesses.

Our team combines decades of Fortune 500 finance leadership at Microsoft, Diageo, BAT, and Boston Scientific with top-tier strategy consulting at Bain & Company. Together, we bring deep experience across technology, FMCG, medical devices, luxury & beverages, materials, education, and financial services.
