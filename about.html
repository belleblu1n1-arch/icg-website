Mark Lea

Lead Advisor International Structuring, Governance & Comparative Taxation

Mark is a respected veteran in international estate planning, structuring, governance, and comparative taxation, with over 50 years of experience across multiple jurisdictions. He began his career with N.M. Rothschild & Sons Limited in London and later became a Director of Rothschild’s Trust Companies and of its Guernsey Bank.

He has advised the Governments of Singapore, Hong Kong, and Malaysia on trust and related laws, and has undertaken drafting, law‑reform, and advisory roles across multiple jurisdictions. He also served as a Member of the Inland Revenue Board of Review of Hong Kong, on the executive committees of STEP Hong Kong and the Hong Kong Trustees Association, and later on their Joint Law Reform Committee.

Mark was Adjunct Professor of Law at the National University of Singapore for 10 years, teaching international estate planning, including the use of trusts and foundations. He brings exceptional depth, technical judgement, and longstanding credibility to Island Capital’s international structuring, governance, and comparative taxation work.
