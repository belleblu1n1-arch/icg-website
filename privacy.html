Privacy Notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Island Capital Pte. Ltd. (“Island Capital”, “we”) respects your privacy. This notice explains how we collect, use and protect personal data in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

We collect only the personal data you choose to give us, for example when you:

  • complete our enquiry form (name, email, company, phone, and the details of your enquiry);
  • subscribe to our insights (email address and any topics you select);
  • contact us by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn.

How we use it

  • to respond to your enquiry and discuss how we may work together;
  • to provide our services if you become a client;
  • to send you insights, if you have subscribed;
  • to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

Our website forms are processed by Formspree, which delivers your submission to our email. We also use standard email and cloud services to run our business. These providers handle data on our behalf and may store it outside Singapore; we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a comparable standard of protection.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, and then delete it securely.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from our insights at any time, withdraw your consent, or ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you. Just email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact us

For any question about this notice or your personal data, please contact our data protection contact at hello@islandcapital.com.sg.
