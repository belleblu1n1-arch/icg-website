Fractional CFO in Singapore: questions answered

What a fractional CFO does, how engagements work, and company set-up in Singapore.

About fractional CFO support

Who is Island Capital?

Island Capital Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based firm providing fractional CFO leadership, regional Finance and Compliance, and structuring, set-up and corporate services for businesses expanding and operating in Asia‑Pacific. It is an ACRA-registered Corporate Service Provider.

What is a fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO is an experienced finance leader who works with your business part of the time, as and when you need it, instead of as a full-time employee. You get senior finance leadership for planning, forecasting, cash, reporting, board support and decision-making, scaled to the stage and seasonality of your business.

Is a fractional CFO the same as an outsourced or part-time CFO?

The terms are often used interchangeably. All describe senior CFO-level leadership provided without a full-time hire. At Island Capital, fractional CFO support runs as an ongoing partnership or on a project basis, flexible in time and scope.

When does a fractional CFO make more sense than a full-time CFO?

A fractional CFO suits businesses that need senior finance judgement but not a full-time executive: growing companies, owner-led businesses, groups setting up or running regional operations, and companies going through a transition such as a CFO change, integration or restructuring. As the business grows, the role can expand, or support a full-time hire when the time is right.

What does a fractional CFO do for a business in Singapore and Asia‑Pacific?

Typical areas include planning, forecasting and cash; monthly performance reviews; reporting and board materials; decision support and insight; integration, transition and change; and, for regional groups, one consistent set of numbers across every market. See the areas we support.

Engagements and fees

How much does a fractional CFO cost?

Fees depend on scope and time. You pay for the leadership you need rather than a full-time salary. Project-basis work is priced for a defined scope, Regional Finance & Compliance runs monthly at a fixed fee, and company set-up is a fixed fee. The first conversation is free, and we confirm scope and fee before any work starts.

How are engagements structured?

Fractional CFO support is an ongoing partnership, or a project around a specific need, typically 4–12 weeks. Regional Finance & Compliance runs monthly. There is no long-term lock-in.

Do you replace our accountant or bookkeeper?

No. We provide finance leadership: planning, decisions, performance and reporting that leadership teams and boards rely on. We work alongside your accountant, bookkeeper and auditors, and coordinate across finance, tax, legal, and compliance.

How quickly can an engagement begin?

Most engagements start within two weeks of agreement. Project-basis engagements can begin sooner, depending on scope.

Singapore and the region

Can you help set up a company in Singapore?

Yes. Island Capital is an ACRA-registered Corporate Service Provider. We handle incorporation, company secretary, annual return and statutory filing, local director arrangements and a registered office address in Singapore, and design the entity and operating structure for regional operations.

Which markets do you cover?

We are based in Singapore and work with businesses across Asia‑Pacific, from the regional HQ to every subsidiary. Incorporation and corporate services are provided in Singapore; finance leadership, compliance governance and structuring cover the wider region.
